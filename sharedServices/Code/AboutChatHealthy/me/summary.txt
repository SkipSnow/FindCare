## ChatHealthy.ai
chatHealthy.ai LLC is a Delaware LLC, officially founded on March 26, 2026.

chatHealthy.ai is a consumer healthcare navigation platform that uses artificial intelligence to tell individuals not just where they can seek reimbursable care — but which of their available options delivers the highest quality care. It does this using only publicly available data, assembled and reasoned over by an agentic AI system that understands each consumer's unique context: their insurance, location, access to transportation, and health situation.

This is disruptive by design. Insurance companies, healthcare providers, and pharmaceutical companies have a structural incentive to keep both quality and cost information opaque. Consumers today make some of the most consequential decisions of their lives — which doctor to see, which hospital to use, which treatment path to pursue — with almost no actionable information. chatHealthy.ai changes that.

The platform consists of three components:
- Find Care — find providers, specialties, navigate the system. Free forever. This is the funnel.
- Evaluate Care — clinical trials, provider credentials, quality metrics. Trust builder. Free now, paywall post-trust.
- Discuss Care — multi-participant AI-assisted healthcare collaboration. Revenue engine. Premium token markup + vendor partnerships.

Mission: Help people make better healthcare decisions together.

chatHealthy.ai is intentionally designed as a one-person company with an agentic operational footprint. This is not a limitation — it is a structural competitive advantage and a proof of concept for what the AI moment makes possible. The founder architects, supervises, and directs — the agents execute.

Revenue model: No care-chain revenue (GOV-001: Provider Neutrality). Revenue from AI token markup in Discuss Care sessions and AI vendor partnerships. AI companies pay to participate in high-value healthcare decision contexts. Users retain control and pay only for optional premium AI engagement.
