## Anthropic Operating Principles — Extracted from Co-Founder Conversation

SOURCE: "Building Anthropic | A Conversation with Our Co-Founders" (YouTube)

1. SAFETY AND CAPABILITY ARE INSEPARABLE. Scaling AI and making it safe are not competing goals — they are the same goal. RLHF and alignment require capable models. Safety research is done through the same work that advances capability.

2. BET AGAINST CONSENSUS WHEN THE EVIDENCE WARRANTS IT. Herding behavior masquerades as maturity. If the data supports a counter-consensus view, make the bet and don't ask permission from the field. Being right 50% of the time when no one else is betting adds enormous value.

3. SIMPLE THINGS WORK SURPRISINGLY WELL. Constitutional AI worked because AI systems are good at evaluating themselves against clear principles. When you can define a clear target, the model will hit it. Complexity is rarely the answer.

4. TAKE THE RISK SERIOUSLY BEFORE OTHERS DO. The founders studied nuclear physics history — Szilard and Teller took the atomic bomb seriously before consensus caught up. The same applies to AI. If it could be a big deal, treat it like a big deal now.

5. PUBLISH CONSTANTLY. Shifting the field requires publishing. Anthropic's safety research is a constant force — the goal is not just to build safe AI internally but to move the entire field's understanding of what safety problems actually are.

6. BUILD FOR THE LONG-TERM MISSION, NOT NEAR-TERM VALIDATION. Anthropic was founded knowing it would be a "slightly strange, safety-oriented thing." The founders accepted that the mission would not be universally understood or validated immediately.

7. INTERPRETABILITY IS FOUNDATIONAL. Understanding what is happening inside AI systems — not just what they output — is a core research commitment. You cannot align what you cannot understand.

8. THE GOAL IS BENEFICIAL AI FOR HUMANITY. Not AI for Anthropic. Not AI for revenue. The mission is to build AI that is safe and beneficial for humanity as a whole — a bet-the-company level commitment that shapes every product and research decision.

9. LANGUAGE MODELS AS AN ALIGNMENT TOOL. A core founding insight: language models must understand implicit human knowledge to function. This makes them inherently more alignable than earlier AI paradigms. The bet on LLMs was partly a safety bet.

10. HUMAN SUPERVISION IS A PHASE, NOT THE END STATE. The current human-in-the-loop model is a validation step. The architecture is designed for a future where AI systems can operate more autonomously — but only after sufficient alignment work has been done.
